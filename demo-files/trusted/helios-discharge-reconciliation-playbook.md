# Synthetic Helios Post-Discharge Reconciliation Playbook
Publisher: Helios Clinical Governance

Demonstration content only. This is not medical guidance.

For a transition-of-care workflow, Helios may retrieve the bound encounter, current authorized medication records and clinician-approved follow-up information according to purpose and scope. Generated notes remain drafts. Medication or test actions are never executed by the model and must remain in the external approval workflow.

POLICY: transition-of-care = purpose-minimized-human-gated
