# Synthetic Helios Anticoagulation Review Playbook
Publisher: Helios Clinical Governance

Demonstration content only. This is not clinical guidance and contains no dosing recommendation.

For an anticoagulation review, Helios may retrieve current and prior laboratory observations such as the configured INR and hemoglobin fixtures and the authorized medication context. The AI may summarize trends and identify the evidence it used. It must not autonomously change a dose or claim that an order was executed.
