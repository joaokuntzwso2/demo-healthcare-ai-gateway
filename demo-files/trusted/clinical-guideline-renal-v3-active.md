# Helios Renal Medication Review Guideline
VERSION: 3.0
STATUS: ACTIVE
TOPIC: renal-medication-guideline

For the synthetic Helios medication-review workflow, current renal function must be reviewed from authoritative laboratory evidence before a medication-change discussion. Review current potassium, creatinine and eGFR evidence and preserve corrected-result provenance. AI may summarize evidence but may not autonomously change, prescribe or execute a medication order.
